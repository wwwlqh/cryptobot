### ✅ Label check — the BT grades on what actually paid

`BT (as backtested)` and `gamma (what actually paid)` agree on **100.00% of bets** — the tick replay settles on the point close, the rule these markets really paid on. `legacy`, recomputed independently from our own Chainlink 1s tape, corroborates it. The agreement table below is the check: it should be ~100% in every move-size band, and a band that sags is the signature of a mis-targeted pool.

So the deployed edge stands as backtested: **+0.0705/bet** at 77.27% WR, real **$+39,920**.

### What the 2026-08-07 TWAP switch does to these bets

Close-only flips **21.00%** of our bets (WR 77.27% → 86.98%); symmetric flips **29.58%** (WR → 76.73%). Close-only is a tailwind, symmetric is roughly neutral — matching the AUC arm of the label study.

⛔ **The twap_c / twap_s EV and $ columns are NOT a forecast.** These books were quoted against the point rule, so grading them on a TWAP label harvests the rule gap as edge that cannot survive the venue actually pricing TWAP. What carries over is the **flip rate**, the **WR direction**, and the 5m-vs-15m **asymmetry** — not the dollars.

⚠️ **15m is the exposure that breaks if the OPEN is averaged too.** Under `twap_s` the 15m cells go negative while 5m stays positive (see the table below). The open-side convention is still unannounced; this is the concrete P&L reason to confirm it before 08-07.

## Outputs

This script writes exactly two files: **this digest** and **`best_hold_bets.parquet`** under `BT_RUNS_DIR` — one row per graded bet, carrying `fill`, `pl`, every label column and one `ev::<rule>` column per resolution rule. Every table above is an aggregate of that parquet; re-cut it rather than hunting for a CSV.

## Caveats

- §1 and §2 are the **same bets** (the T3 detail frame), but different units: §1 compounds the bankroll, §2's EV column is a flat $1 stake. §2's `real $` column uses the BT's own booked stakes and fees and is the one directly comparable to §1's P&L.
- Uniform-theta fee is a **model**; the live ledger books fees asymmetrically, so a modelled margin never equals the cash basis exactly.
- No per-bet equity **series** exists for §1 — fold reports only ever stored summary stats. A real 100→N curve needs a re-run of `scripts/tick_topup_foldBE.py`.
